Nodes

Rules match the records. AI explains the gaps. A person signs off.

Reconciliation is the job every finance team knows: ERP, payment provider and bank records each tell a slightly different truth. I designed Nodes so rules do the matching and people spend their time on the exceptions.

So I built it. A deterministic engine does the matching, a reviewer signs off every exception, and an AI layer explains each gap in plain English without ever deciding. Everything leaves an audit trail.

  • Rules match, not the model
  • 993 automated tests, 235 scored
  • AI explains, a reviewer decides

Working Capital Agent

Cash gaps flagged days before they land, with an AI that proposes the fix and a human who approves it.

I wired the whole thing myself in a day. n8n pulls cash movements from four sources, a fixed-rules step forecasts the next 30 days, then K2 Horizon weighs the fix: chase a customer early, or ask a supplier to wait. A second AI step checks the first one's work, and nobody gets an email until a finance manager approves it in Slack.

  • LLM prompting and validation (K2 Horizon)
  • Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail
  • Approval gate design
