In buildFlagship · Full reconciliation platform, built with AI

Nodes

The problem: every month-end I matched thousands of lines by hand. The answer: I built the system.

At Canon and Pearl, reconciliation ate the first week of every month. ERP, the payment provider and the bank never agreed, and the fix was me plus a spreadsheet. I knew what a good reconciliation looked like. I just couldn't code it.

Now I can. Nodes is the platform I designed and built end to end: a deterministic engine does the matching, 18 control checks run over it, a reviewer signs off every exception, and an AI layer explains each gap in plain English without ever deciding. Everything leaves an audit trail.

  • Rules match, not the model
  • 18 controls in the pack
  • AI explains, a reviewer decides

Working Capital Agent

Fourteen n8n steps, K2 Horizon doing the thinking.

I wired the whole thing myself in a day. n8n pulls cash movements from four sources, a fixed-rules step forecasts the next 30 days, then K2 Horizon weighs the fix: chase a customer early, or ask a supplier to wait. A second AI step checks the first one's work, and nobody gets an email until a finance manager approves it in Slack.

For the recruiters: n8n workflow design, prompting and validating an LLM (K2 Horizon), Google Sheets, Slack and Gmail wiring, and an approval gate designed by someone who's actually owned a cash forecast.

  • 14 steps in n8n
  • 30 days forecast ahead
  • 0 emails sent without a yes

02

What I'm good at.

I don't record the entries anymore. I turn what's recorded into a decision, a model or a system. Three things I'd bring, each one proven on a real job.

  1. 01Forecast

    See the cash squeeze weeks early

    Rolling cash forecasts that flag the week a supplier payment leaves before a customer pays, so we can decide who to chase and who to pay first. Scenario versions for "what if the big client pays late" too.

    I run this at LSN today. The CEO plans around a forecast I refresh every week, and it's what caught a 26% cost overrun mid project and made us shorten quote validity.

  2. 02Model

    Models you can defend in the room

    ROI, payback, TCO, NPV/IRR. Built so a CFO or a client can pull them apart line by line. Budget versus actual, margin per project or unit, and the cost structure underneath, not just a top-line answer.

    Ten years of real P&Ls, balance sheets and reconciliations means my assumptions come from how companies actually behave, not how a template says they should.

  3. 03Extract & automate

    Get the data, then automate the grind

    I get what I need straight from the system rather than asking someone for an export. Power BI today, SQL and Python through my CFI work next. And if a process eats the team's week, I rebuild the process.

    That's how Canon's commission report went from 30 hours to about 3, and where the projects on this page came from.
