Nodes

Rules reconcile. AI explains exceptions. Finance decides.

ERP, payment-provider and bank records often disagree in different ways. I designed and built Nodes to reconcile those sources deterministically, route exceptions for review and keep every decision traceable.

Across a range of high-volume reconciliation scenarios covering 1,000+ e-commerce transactions, 95% were automatically reconciled and 5% routed for analyst review, with zero incorrect automated matches.

AI is limited to evidence-grounded exception explanation. Each transaction remains traceable to its source records and reconciliation evidence, with review decisions captured in the audit trail. The analyst retains final approval.

  • 95% automatically reconciled
  • 0 incorrect automated matches
  • 1,000+ transactions assessed

Working Capital Agent

Cash gaps flagged days before they land, with an AI that proposes the fix and a human who approves it.

I wired the whole thing myself in a day. n8n pulls cash movements from four sources, a fixed-rules step forecasts the next 30 days, then K2 Horizon weighs the fix: chase a customer early, or ask a supplier to wait. A second AI step checks the first one's work, and nobody gets an email until a finance manager approves it in Slack.

  • LLM prompting and validation (K2 Horizon)
  • Google Sheets, Slack, Gmail
  • Approval gate design
