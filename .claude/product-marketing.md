# Product marketing context: Fahad Tahir's portfolio

## What we're "selling"
Fahad Tahir, a Dubai finance professional with about 10 years at Canon, Pearl Initiative and LSN Signs, who also builds finance tools with AI. The "product" is Fahad as a hire.

## Page purpose and primary action
Get a finance recruiter or hiring manager to download the CV or message Fahad on LinkedIn about a role. Secondary: open Nodes and see it working.

## Audience
- Dubai agency recruiters (finance desks) and in-house talent teams. Skim on a phone, 30 to 90 seconds, matching against a job description.
- Finance hiring managers: FP&A managers, Financial Controllers, Finance Directors, CFOs. Read numbers carefully. Want someone who owns the close, the forecast and the numbers they present, and who makes the team faster.
- Target roles: FP&A Analyst or Manager, Finance Business Partner, Finance Manager, Accounting Manager, Senior Accountant. Dubai.
- The words they use: FP&A, budgeting, forecasting, variance analysis, business partnering, month-end close, reconciliations, IFRS, audit, internal controls, ERP, Power BI, KPI, management reporting, working capital.
- Where they come from: Fahad's CV and LinkedIn profile, so they already know his job history.

## What makes Fahad different
Most finance candidates show a CV. Fahad shows the work: 10 years of real finance results plus tools he built himself, designed the way a controller would (rules do the maths, AI explains, a person approves).

## Proof (use only these)
- 10 audits end to end at Pearl Initiative (3 statutory, 7 grant), all on time with no findings.
- 13-week rolling cash forecast at LSN Signs, updated monthly; caught an AED 200k gap five weeks early; CEO approved his plan.
- IFRS 15 cost-to-cost WIP schedule at LSN, accepted by the external accounting firm, now part of every close.
- Found a 26% raw-materials overrun at LSN; changed pricing policy with the CEO; re-priced jobs back to an 18 to 22% margin.
- Canon commission reporting moved to Power BI: about 30 hours a month to about 3, five-day lag to during the month, across about USD 2.5M of monthly sales.
- Held quarter-end revenue cut-off on a USD 150k deal at Canon; his pending-installations control was approved by the Financial Controller.
- Finance representative in UAT for Canon's Orion ERP move, 15+ finance defects logged.
- Nodes: working reconciliation prototype; 1,000 synthetic transactions, 950 tied out by rules, 50 flagged for review, 0 wrong answers from the rules; 993 automated tests.
- ACCA F1 to F9 passed, sitting SBR in March 2027. CFI FMVA.

## Voice and tone
Confident, sharp, warm, a little playful. First person. Short sentences mixed with longer ones. Specific numbers over adjectives. Sounds like a smart finance person talking over coffee in Dubai, not a brochure. Play offense: lead with wins, never with apologies or answers to doubts.

## Never
Invent or round up any fact. Use anything in the brief's Section 3. Use em dashes, emojis, or: elevate, revolutionize, leverage, unleash, harness, synergy, ecosystem, paradigm, deep dive, game changer, showcase, highlight, insights, delve, profound, groundbreaking, fostering, "not only X but also Y", "from X to Y", "it's not X, it's Y".
